# Hagn & Partner

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
delegated by the user: modular Next.js App Router with TypeScript, server-rendered public pages, a custom visual CMS, Neon Postgres and Vercel Blob. Vercel deployment with German region requested.

## Users
Prospective and existing clients looking for legal representation in Ingolstadt. Office staff editing the website without design or development experience.

## Product Purpose
Present Hagn, Deckert, Stemmer & Partner PartG mbB, explain its actual legal services, make contact easy, and give office staff direct control of all content.

## Operating Context
Real original content and images from https://www.hagn-partner.de/. Seven named lawyers, original legal fields, office hours, contact data and downloadable forms. New inquiries must be stored in the backend even when SMTP has not been configured.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Responsive public website; password-only administrator access with the initial password supplied privately by the user and changeable in admin; live visual section editor; move, add, remove, duplicate sections; generous reusable section library; image library and nine-point image crop alignment; per-section design controls; drafts, publish, revisions; inquiry inbox; SMTP, consent and analytics settings; onboarding and help. Architecture should allow future locales and features. Never publish secrets. No invented testimonials, credentials or results.

## Brand Commitments
Hagn & Partner, full legal entity Hagn, Deckert, Stemmer & Partner PartG mbB. User explicitly delegates modern, exceptionally appealing design direction after current-site and contemporary design research. All copy uses the firm's perspective and original factual personal tone. No AI clichés, staging instructions, fictional commercial claims. Dedicated copy-review subagent required by user.

## Evidence on Hand
Original website, portraits, service descriptions, legal notice, privacy notice, office contact and forms. Scraped evidence in docs/source and asset provenance in docs/content-inventory.md. No client reviews or case outcomes supplied.

## Product Principles
- Give clients a clear next step without making legal promises.
- Make edits understandable, previewable and reversible.
- Store and process submitted information securely.
- Use genuine content and images, optimize for search and machine readability without keyword stuffing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Desktop and mobile, keyboard usable, meaningful focus, clear contrast, touch-friendly controls and reduced-motion support.
